The pinnacle

of wellbeing

Touching the sky at over 100 metres high, the ‘Sky Amenities Deck’ is an architectural and experiential feature with a spectacular infinity pool, double-height gym, yoga studio and lounge areas, all overlooking the stunning vistas that surround.

These are complimented by additional amenities on the podium levels, including indoor pools, spa and meeting rooms, as well as direct access to VELA and The Lana Hotel amenities via the waterfront promenade.
